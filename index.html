Astro.log.io

Astro.log.io integrates art, science, technology and ancestral knowledge, expanding the concept of the music of the spheres. It crafts personalized immersive experiences from individual natal charts: combining artificial intelligence, astronomical data and astrological meanings to generate unique soundscapes that reflect the position, motion and characteristics of celestial bodies at a specific date and time.

Inspired by Pythagoras' "music of the spheres" and Kepler's Harmonices Mundi — the idea that planetary motions follow harmonic proportions. A medieval root of the Quadrivium: arithmetic, geometry, music and astronomy.

Artificial intelligence Astronomy Astrology Virtual reality Augmented reality Apps Immersive installation

Formats

Mobile apps · online platforms · virtual and augmented reality · physical installations · culminating in a large-scale immersive environment.

Launch

April 2024 · Now Play This, experimental design festival, Somerset House, London. Curated by Luján Oulton. Coverage in The Guardian. Over 700 visitors experienced the Alpha version.
